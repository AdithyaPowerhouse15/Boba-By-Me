Introduction

Bubble tea (or boba) is a beloved drink that originated in Taiwan in the early 1980s. It typically consists of a tea base mixed with milk or fruit, topped with chewy tapioca pearls (boba).

Popular Boba Flavors

  • Classic Milk Tea with Tapioca Pearls
  • Taro Milk Tea
  • Matcha Green Tea Latte
  • Brown Sugar Fresh Milk
  • Mango Fruit Tea with Coconut Jelly
